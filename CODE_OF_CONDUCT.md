# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment. Contributors should focus on the work, assume good faith, and welcome constructive technical disagreement.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, provide actionable feedback, protect private information, and respect different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sexualized conduct, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
